Formulas dropped in this directory are the experiment rig's formulas_dir.

Gas City gives that directory priority over pack formulas for this rig only,
matched by filename. A website-factory.toml here replaces the factory pack's
formula when the experiment rig runs it, and leaves every other rig on the
pack formula. The directory ships empty of toml: the variants in
../variants.tsv already name the pack and city formulas.
